---
title: Why your first SOC job will not look like the courses
date: 2026-08-12
read_time: 7 min
---

The gap between training material and a real security operations centre is mostly about volume and ambiguity. Courses give you one alert with one answer. A shift gives you two hundred alerts, most of which are noise generated by your own estate.

What actually carries you through the first six months is not tool knowledge. It is the habit of writing down what you checked, in order, so that the next person can pick your work up mid-thought. Everything else is learnable on the job.

If you are preparing now, practise the boring part: take one alert a day from a home lab, triage it end to end, and write a three-paragraph verdict. Do that for a month and you will interview better than someone with two more certificates.
